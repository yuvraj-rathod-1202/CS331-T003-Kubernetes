## Honest Summary

The AI acted as an expert pair programmer across all phases. It generated boilerplate code, fixed bugs after I described them, and produced documentation. The significant decisions were mine:

- **Architecture choices**: reconciliation strategy, failure thresholds, cooldown logic design — I caught the race condition and the infinite loop risk independently.
- **Cluster management**: every `kubectl`, `helm`, and `minikube` command was run and verified by me in my local environment.
- **Testing**: all end-to-end testing (CoreDNS degradation, NetworkPolicy drift, visualizer behavior) was executed and interpreted by me.
- **Code review**: every AI-generated file was reviewed before being accepted into the codebase.

The **Prompt-Refiner** tool (see `Tools.md`) was also part of the pipeline — it structured my rough prompts into detailed, precise specifications before they reached the AI model, which improved output quality significantly.
